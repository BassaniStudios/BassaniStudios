SITE GOAL
---------
Showcase for photos and videos from Bassani Studios.
Digital studio focused on GTA-related cinematic content.
Audience: gamers who love digital art.
Tone: friendly.


TECHNOLOGY
----------
- HTML5 + CSS3 + Vanilla JavaScript
- No frameworks (lightweight, easy to host)
- Fonts: Orbitron + Rajdhani (Google Fonts)
- Fully responsive


PAGE STRUCTURE
--------------
1. index.html           → Home (hero + gallery previews + slides + 3D banner)
2. photo-studio.html    → Photo gallery (placeholders ready for your links)
3. video-studio.html    → Video Studio grid (24 YouTube videos)
4. crews-videos.html    → Crew Studio grid (24 YouTube videos)
5. bassani-studios.html → Brand page
6. video-player.html    → Dedicated player with both lists + iframe
7. about.html           → About the studio + social links
