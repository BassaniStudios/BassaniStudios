VIRTUAL DANCEFLOOR

Music Locker Track

Experience the forbidden thrill of dancing to banging house and techno with hundreds of tightly packed strangers at the newly-opened Music Locker, the hottest club in Los Santos. Underground in every possible way, The Music Locker is located directly beneath The Diamond Casino & Resort, offering the pure ecstasy of a sweaty all-nighter on the dancefloor with the world’s best DJs playing on a world class sound system, along with the comfort of special benefits for The Diamond’s Penthouse-owning VIPs.

Inside the Locker

VIP nights, residencies and the sounds that keep Los Santos moving

VIP · Bottle Service

Behind the Velvet Rope

Share bottle service behind the velvet rope with some of the most important people in Los Santos, or unwind with drink at either of the two bars. The party never has to end – unless you need an ambulance. Spray the champagne, but keep some water at the table.

Residency

Palms Trax

Known for bringing deep and peaceful, positive vibes to any party, Palms Trax’s mix of old-school house, exotic strains of disco and the latest from the UK underground will fill floors around the clock with his newly announced residency at The Music Locker. Get ready to float away to your own personal paradise on the dancefloor.

Legend

Moodymann

Legendary DJ, producer and record label owner, Kenny Dixon Jr. aka Moodymann is a true legend in dance music. Built around his genre-spanning vinyl sets based on the raw club sounds of the industrial Midwest, Moodymann and his back dancers take the party people on an elevated journey through sound, space and time.

Collective

KEiNEMUSIk

Keinemusik is a creative collective focused on forward-thinking electronic music of all kinds. Hailing from Berlin and recent arrivals to Los Santos, Adam Port, &Me and Rampa have quickly built a reputation for hosting the most exclusive parties in Los Santos and beyond. Whether it’s late night sets at The Music Locker or beach parties on private Caribbean islands, Keinemusik know how to keep a crowd moving.
